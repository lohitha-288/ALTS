I am excited to share my latest project as part of my data analytics internship! 📊✨
I built a fully interactive Telco Customer Churn Dashboard in Power BI designed to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights. This dashboard provides executive-level tracking at a glance, highlighting critical retention and business health metrics.
Key Features Built:
🔹 3 Dynamic KPI Cards summarizing essential metrics (Total Accounts, Churned Accounts, and Churn Rate %).
🔹 Count of Accounts by Churn Flag Donut Chart for an immediate view of customer losses.
🔹 Count of Accounts by Plan Tier Bar Chart to visualize churn variations across subscription groups (Pro, Basic, Enterprise).
🔹 Churn Rate by Month Line Chart to seamlessly track and monitor retention trends over time.
This project helped me master advanced UI/UX visual formatting, managing axis and label text hierarchies for better data readability, and creating clean layouts in Power BI. Open to any feedback from the data community! 📈
